05Usage Rules

Do

  • Use the gold mark on navy, or the navy-tile mark on light backgrounds — never gold on white without the tile
  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the "H" in the wordmark
  • Use the icon alone only at small sizes (favicon, avatar, app icon); pair with the wordmark everywhere else
  • Keep .PH in gold in every lockup — it's part of the brand name

Don't

  • Don't add gradients, shadows, or outlines to the mark — flat only
  • Don't recolor the mark teal or white-on-gold; gold-on-navy is the identity
  • Don't stretch, rotate, or set the wordmark in a different font
  • Don't place the mark over busy photography without the navy tile behind it

06Recommendation & Next Steps

Recommended path: Concept A (Hub Frame monogram) as the primary mark, paired with Concept C's wordmark as the full lockup. It's the most distinctive at every size, it encodes both "EstateHub" initials and a building grid, and it avoids the two crowded clichés of the category — literal rooftops and shields. Keep Concept D's shield only as a small "verified/secure" badge inside the product, not as the logo.
