What emerald says

  • Growth, land, prosperity — natural fit for "building generational wealth"
  • More ownable: no major PH portal uses emerald-on-navy (green players like MyProperty use lighter, brighter greens)
  • Reads modern/fintech — closer to a proptech product than a classic brokerage

What gold says

  • Luxury, premium service, established trust — matches your current homepage exactly
  • Warmer against the navy; highest perceived "wealth" signal
  • Zero rework: your mockup's CTAs, dividers, and .PH are already gold
One thing to decide: the logo color should also become the site's accent color. If you go emerald, the homepage's gold CTAs, dividers, and .PH should switch to emerald too — mixing an emerald logo with gold buttons will look unintentional. A workable middle path exists (emerald as the brand mark and primary accent, keeping gold only as a small "premium listing" badge color), but the two shouldn't share the same screen at equal weight.
